Smaller PDF

An iPhone and iPad app · Last updated 14 August 2026

Smaller makes PDFs small enough to send — a 27.8 MB slide deck becomes 2.1 MB, with text still sharp and selectable. It works from the share sheet in Mail and Files, and everything happens on your device: no account, no upload, no subscription.
